Doorsteps Property Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Introduction

Doorsteps Property ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Doorsteps Property application.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, the app may handle:

  • Account and sign-in information.
  • Client names and contact details you enter.
  • Property details, listings and transaction records.
  • Viewing details and property-related service requests.
  • Mortgage and affordability calculations and financial figures you enter.
  • Electronic signature images and related signing details.
  • Technical information required by the app or its supporting services.

Please only enter information necessary for your use of the app and ensure you have permission to enter any information relating to other people.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide the app's property-management features.
  • Manage client, property and transaction records.
  • Support property viewings and related workflows.
  • Perform mortgage and affordability calculations.
  • Record electronic signatures when the feature is used.
  • Prepare communications that you choose to share.
  • Help maintain the security and operation of the app.

4. Data Storage

Information entered into the app may be stored locally in your browser or on your device. Locally stored information may remain there until it is deleted or the relevant browser or device data is cleared. Clearing that data may result in the loss of saved information.

Locally stored information may not be backed up or synchronised across devices. You should protect access to your device and take care when using the app on a shared device.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

When you choose to use a sharing feature, such as opening WhatsApp with a prepared message, information included in that message may be shared with the third-party service you select. Review the message before sending it.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is subject to those policies and practices.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect legal rights or safety. If the app sends personal information to hosting providers, analytics services, referral partners or other organisations, those disclosures must be described accurately in this policy.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The app may use browser local storage to save information and settings. Local storage is different from a traditional cookie. You can manage or clear locally stored data through your browser settings, although doing so may affect app functionality or remove saved records.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it or clear the relevant browser or app data. You should delete information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Information shared with third-party services may be retained according to those services' own retention policies.

8. Data Security

We recommend keeping your device and software updated, using appropriate device security, and restricting access to client and property records. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, object to certain processing, or complain to a data protection authority.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's Privacy

Doorsteps Property is intended for property-related use and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app, our data practices or applicable legal requirements. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
